Our preprint *[PQLN: Post-Quantum Security for the Bitcoin Lightning Network's Off-Chain Surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13781)* is now on arXiv. To our knowledge, it is the first post-quantum design, implementation, and evaluation for the Lightning Network. PQLN nodes can co-exist with the existing Lightning nodes in the network, and node operators can start upgrading today.
